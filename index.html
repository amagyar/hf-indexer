Hugging Face Model Indexer

Search and filter every model on the Hugging Face Hub by size, format, license, and date — right in your browser.

A free, client-side search engine for the Hugging Face Hub. Browse and filter around three million models — transformers, safetensors, GGUF, AWQ, GPTQ, compressed-tensors, bitsandbytes, MLX, BitNet, and ONNX — by parameter count, weight format, license (Apache-2.0, MIT, Llama, and more), downloads, and created or modified date. Queries run locally in your browser via DuckDB WebAssembly over Parquet; there is no backend and no tracking. Use it to find small models for local inference, filter by open license, or discover the most downloaded open-source large language models.
